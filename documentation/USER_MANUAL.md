# EduSense AI 360 — User Manual

EduSense AI 360 monitors classroom engagement from your webcam and turns it into
live insights and exportable reports. This guide covers day-to-day use.

## The interface
The app opens with a tabbed dashboard:

- **Live Monitor** — the webcam feed with an engagement overlay, key metrics, and
  per-student remarks.
- **Analytics** — engagement, participation, and emotion graphs for the session.
- **Student Insights** — per-student behaviour patterns (observational, supportive).
- **Teacher Insights** — constructive, classroom-level observations and suggestions.
- **Reports** — export the session as PDF, Excel, or CSV.
- **Settings** — cameras, frame rate, AI thresholds, and appearance.

## Running a session
1. Open the **Live Monitor** tab and allow camera access in your browser.
2. Type a **session name** (e.g. "Class 8B — Algebra").
3. Press **Start session**. The overlay begins showing engagement per student.
4. Watch the KPI strip: classroom **engagement**, students in frame, distracted
   count, dominant **mood**, FPS, and the **session timer**.
5. Use **Pause / Stop & save** as needed. Stopping saves the session automatically.

### Reading the live view
- Each face is boxed and labelled with its engagement and dominant emotion; the box
  colour shifts green→red as engagement falls.
- The **student remarks** panel gives one supportive line per student.
- A `ZZZ` marker appears if a student's eyes stay closed (possible drowsiness).

## Analytics
Open **Analytics** and press **Refresh analytics** to see:
- the **engagement timeline** (with distraction overlay),
- **participation** over time,
- the **emotion distribution**, and
- **per-student average engagement**.

## Insights
- **Student Insights** describe each student's pattern (e.g. "Consistent, highly
  focused", "Improving engagement"). These describe *observed behaviour only* — they
  are never medical or psychological judgements.
- **Teacher Insights** analyse how the *class* responded and offer constructive
  suggestions (e.g. "Attention dipped around 20m — a short activity can help"). They
  never evaluate or rank the teacher.

## Reports
Open **Reports**:
1. Press **Refresh preview** to see what will be exported.
2. Choose **Export PDF**, **Export Excel**, or **Export CSV**.
3. Use the **Download** control to save the file. Past files appear under **History**.

A report includes the date/time, duration, engagement and attention statistics,
emotion statistics, teacher and student remarks, charts, and an overall summary.

## Settings
Adjust the camera device and frame rate, the emotion backend, appearance theme, and
AI thresholds (eye-open sensitivity, gaze tolerance, distraction threshold, and the
prolonged-inattention duration). Invalid values are rejected with a message; press
**Reset to defaults** to restore shipped settings.

## Privacy
All processing is local. EduSense performs no identity recognition and keeps no
facial-recognition database — it measures engagement patterns for educational use.
